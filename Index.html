How I work

Evidence-based therapy, with room for the whole person.

My approach is collaborative and integrative. We look not only at symptoms, but also at the experiences, relationships, needs and patterns underneath them.

Trauma-informed & EMDR

I am Certified in EMDR and offer Attachment-Focused and IFS-informed EMDR for clients working with trauma and complex histories. I am also certified in Therapeutic Fly-Fishing EMDR, a nature-based modification using Japanese tenkara rods.

Integrative & practical

Depending on what you need, our work may draw from Internal Family Systems, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, CBT, mindfulness-based relapse prevention, and interpersonal and attachment-focused approaches.

About Alex

Rooted in Wilmington. Curious about people.

I was born and raised in Wilmington, NC and am drawn to serving in my hometown. I attended UNC-Chapel Hill School of Social Work in 2014, earning my MSW with a clinical focus and a certificate in Substance Abuse Studies.

I completed my clinical internship at Duke University Counseling and Psychological Services and later worked for more than three years at Hoy Recovery Program, a residential treatment program in rural Northern New Mexico. That work deepened my understanding of how trauma, unmet needs, social forces and history can sit beneath the outer layers of mental illness and addiction.

I've been engaged in self-inquiry, mindfulness and meditation practices for more than 20 years. Outside of work, I run, make music, spend time in nature, and hang out with my partner and our chihuahua-mix.

I am licensed for clinical social work in North Carolina and New Mexico and can provide telemental health services in South Carolina.
